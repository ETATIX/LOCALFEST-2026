Tentang Acara

A Celebration of Creative Minds

Where Culture Meets Creativity

Having successfully ignited the creative spirit across Jakarta, Solo, and Medan since 2022, LOCALFEST continues to be a platform for creative enthusiasts to connect, collaborate, and discover emerging local talents.

LOCALFEST is a vibrant festival that celebrates the best of Indonesia's young creative generation. Featuring fashion pop-up stores, music performances, and culinary experiences, LOCALFEST brings creative minds together in one dynamic space.
